More than a directory: a comprehensive ministry

IDCLATAM not only connects churches but also drives multiple service areas

Church Directory

Free registration of Christ congregations throughout Latin America, helping brothers and sisters find a church faithful to Scripture.

"The King's Little Lambs" Children's Ministry

Led by sister Ana Amador, offering lessons, songs, activities, and PDF resources for children's biblical teaching.

Women's Ministry

A space for edification, prayer, and support for sisters, with Bible studies and spiritual strengthening gatherings.

Preaching Ministry

Support for preachers and teachers to share the gospel, with resources and dissemination of their sermons and studies.

Free Websites

We create complete, responsive websites for churches that don't yet have an online presence, at no cost.

Biblical Resources

Digital library with studies, sermons, reading plans, and free downloadable materials.
